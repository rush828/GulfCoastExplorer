## 💡 **Pro Tips**

### **Voice Search Optimization:**
Since you now support Siri and Alexa:
- ✅ Your business names are clear and pronounceable
- ✅ Addresses are complete and accurate
- ✅ Phone numbers are formatted correctly
- ✅ Categories are conversational ("fishing charter" not just "charter")

### **Social Search Optimization:**
With TikTok support:
- ✅ Keep content fresh (social platforms prioritize recent)
- ✅ Use trending keywords in descriptions
- ✅ Include location tags consistently
- ✅ Mobile-optimized (TikTok users are mobile-first)

### **Privacy Search Optimization:**
For Brave and privacy-focused users:
- ✅ Fast loading (privacy users hate trackers that slow sites)
- ✅ Clean design (no intrusive ads)
- ✅ Respect DNT (Do Not Track) headers
- ✅ Clear privacy policy
