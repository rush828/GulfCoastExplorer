## 🆘 **Troubleshooting**

### **Problem: Not seeing AI bot visits**
**Solution:** Wait 2-3 weeks. Check that `robots.txt` is accessible at root domain.

### **Problem: AI metadata not loading**
**Solution:** Verify `public/.well-known/ai.json` is in the public folder and accessible at `/.well-known/ai.json`.

### **Problem: No AI referral traffic yet**
**Solution:** Normal! Can take 1-2 months. Keep content fresh and accurate.

### **Problem: AI says outdated information**
**Solution:** Update your content. AI engines cache information and need fresh data to update.
